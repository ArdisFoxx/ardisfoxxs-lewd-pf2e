# ArdisFoxx's Lewd Roleplaying (AFLR)

An adult (18+) expansion for Foundry VTT, adding sexual game mechanics to your table: arousal, positions, cumflation, kinks, transformation, pregnancy, spells, creatures, loot and a full audio layer.

You already own this. What follows is how to set it up.

---

## Install

1. In Foundry setup, open the **Modules** tab and click **Install Module**.
2. Paste the manifest URL:
   ```
   https://github.com/ArdisFoxx/ardisfoxxs-lewd-pf2e/releases/latest/download/module.json
   ```
3. Launch your world and enable **ArdisFoxx's Lewd Roleplaying** in Manage Modules.


---

## First-time setup

**1. Set your Lewd Level.** In Module Settings, pick a Lewd Level from 1 to 4. This is the single most important setting: it controls how much of the module is live, from background flavour up to full mid-combat mechanics. Agree it with your table in Session Zero.

**2. Import the essentials, and only those.** From the module compendiums, import the **Arousal System** items and the **Core Macros** into your world. Leave everything else in the compendiums until you need it - importing fewer documents keeps your world loading fast, and the macros pull content out of the compendiums on demand anyway.

**3. Prepare your actors.** The bundled monsters in the actors compendium are already configured and work with every macro out of the box. For anything else - a monster you dragged in, or a custom NPC - select its token and run **Token Initialize** before using module macros on it.

**4. Find the character sheet tab.** Open a character sheet and click the pink heart icon. Arousal, kinks, anatomy, pregnancy, size training and toy settings all live there.

**5. Read the guide.** A full guide journal ships in the module compendiums, covering the expanded rules in their entirety with an example of play, plus a GM screen journal for quick reference at the table.

## Audio

Voice and ambient SFX ship separately in the free **AFLR Soundpack**. The module runs fine without it and includes a basic audio set; the Soundpack expands that considerably, with voice profiles for female, male and monster, plus the position-aware SFX the engine uses. Install it into your Foundry `Data/modules` folder, restart, and enable it - it is detected automatically. You can also point the module at your own folder of custom profiles.

Audio is drawn from the OpenNSFW Sound Pack (CC BY 4.0). Credits ship with the Soundpack; direct queries to @OpenNSFWSP rather than to individual contributors.

Real-life toy support is available through the **AFLR Lovense Integration** package.
